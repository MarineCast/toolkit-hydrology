  P10/P90 indicators are retrospective and model-ineligible.

- Benchmark and budget larger UTC/H3 acquisitions and processing separately from the
  completed bounded native benchmark. Canonical staging budgets do not bound HTTP

- Define an observed-data archive/freshness and recent-revision reconciliation policy,
  including rights/redistribution review. The Explorer read-only adapter is implemented;

## Prioritized hydrology data gaps — planning request, 2026-10-18

Planning assumption supplied for this assessment: discharge is the only confirmed
production-ready measurement. This is not a new toolkit-wide readiness finding and
does not mean the other variables are absent from every source adapter. Existing
partial temperature collection and the coverage work above remain relevant.

These priorities reflect proposed predictive relevance, availability and implementation
effort. They are not measured SRKW feature-importance rankings. All items below are
unfinished qualification/implementation work; no acquisition or source verification
was performed when adding this planning material.

| Missing or insufficiently qualified data | Example model variables | Potential value for SRKW modeling (hypothesis) | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| River water temperature | Mean/max temperature, thermal anomalies, warm-water duration | Salmon migration conditions and thermal stress | P0 |
| Additional river and coastal inflows | Freshwater flow by outlet, watershed and receiving marine basin | More complete representation of freshwater entering whale habitat | P0 |
| Watershed characteristics | Drainage area, elevation, glacier cover, upstream lake area, regulation status | Interpret and compare river discharge and runoff | P0 |
| Snowpack and snowmelt | Snow water equivalent (SWE), snow depth, melt rate, snowmelt runoff | Spring freshet dynamics and possible leading indicators of river conditions | P1 |
| Dam and reservoir operations | Outflow, spill, reservoir storage, release changes | Distinguish natural flow conditions from managed river flows | P1 |
| Turbidity and sediment | Turbidity, suspended sediment concentration, sediment load | Possible proxy for sediment-rich freshwater inputs and plume conditions | P1 |
| River water levels | Stage, maximum daily level, rapid stage changes | Flood conditions; potentially limited additional value beyond discharge | P2 |
| Soil moisture and catchment runoff | Soil moisture, surface runoff, subsurface runoff | Antecedent wetness and potential leading indicators of discharge | P2 |
| Water chemistry | Conductivity, dissolved oxygen, pH, nutrients | Freshwater quality and possible ecological stressors | P2 |
| Groundwater and baseflow | Groundwater levels, baseflow contribution | Low-flow conditions and delayed watershed responses | P3 |

### Candidate historical sources to qualify

The following descriptions and archive dates are user-supplied research leads, not
newly verified coverage claims. Check station/parameter-specific completeness across
the intended 2009–2026 training period, bulk access, units, sampling cadence, source
flags, gaps, revisions/availability and use rights. Overall archive dates do not
establish usable coverage at each station. Retain observed versus modeled identity;
do not turn discrete samples or unknown coverage into daily observations.

- [ ] DFO Fraser River Environmental Watch: qualify Fraser temperature, including
  Qualark. The supplied lead reports historical reference records extending to 1950;
  published reports demonstrate observations, but bulk historical access needs
  qualification. Assess salmon-related temperature features without assuming
  complete station history.
  https://www.pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/science/habitat/frw-rfo/reports-rapports/2026/2026-07-20-eng.html
- [ ] Columbia River DART: assess river temperature, reservoir outflow/spill,
  elevation, dissolved gas and turbidity. The supplied lead reports an overall
  river-environment archive extending to 1949; establish actual parameter/station
  coverage rather than applying that start date to every variable.
  https://www.cbr.washington.edu/dart/overview
- [ ] Washington Ecology Freshwater DataStream and Freshwater Information Network:
  qualify streamflow, temperature, dissolved oxygen, pH and conductivity at monitored
  stations, and distinguish historical discrete water-quality observations from
  continuous series.
  https://ecology.wa.gov/research-data/monitoring-assessment/water-quality-monitoring
- [ ] Water Survey of Canada HYDAT: investigate historical water levels, discharge
  and sediment access beyond real-time API retention. Qualify the reported archive
  breadth separately for each station/parameter; daily means do not reconstruct UTC
  unit-value observations.
  https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/water-overview/quantity/monitoring/survey/data-products-services/national-archive-hydat.html
- [ ] BC Snow Survey and NRCS SNOTEL: inventory historical SWE and snow-depth records
  and qualify station coverage and basin-level representation for snowpack features.
  https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-science-data/water-data-tools/snow-survey-data
  https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/resources/data-and-reports/snow-and-climate-monitoring-predefined-reports-and-maps
- [ ] NOAA National Water Model Retrospective: assess modeled streamflow and
  hydrological land-surface variables in the US domain. The supplied lead gives
  version 3.0 coverage as February 1979–January 2023; verify that window and plan
  coverage beyond it. That version alone cannot establish a consistent 2009–2026
  product. Keep modeled runoff distinct from gauge observations and qualify any
  version/source combination explicitly.
  https://registry.opendata.aws/nwm-archive/
